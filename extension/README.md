# 🧩 Udemy Course Finder Assistant (Browser Extension)

A lightweight Chromium & Firefox extension to 1-click batch enroll in fresh Udemy coupons directly from your Udemy Course Finder dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Installation (1 Minute)

1. Open your browser's extension manager:
   - **Chrome / Brave**: `chrome://extensions`
   - **Edge**: `edge://extensions`
2. Turn **ON** the **"Developer mode"** toggle (usually in top-right or sidebar).
3. Click **"Load unpacked"**.
4. Select this directory: `/media/akai/NFORCE/Udemy Course Finder/extension`
5. Pin the **Udemy Course Finder Assistant** icon to your browser toolbar!

---

## ⚡ How to Use

1. Ensure you are **logged into Udemy** (`udemy.com`) in your browser.
2. Click the extension icon on your toolbar.
3. You'll see:
   - **Today's Free**: Fresh courses discovered today.
   - **Untouched**: Courses you haven't opened yet.
4. Click:
   - **⚡ 1-Click Auto-Enroll All**: Automatically opens each course in the background, redeems the 100% discount, and closes the tab.
   - **🌐 Open All in Tabs**: Opens up to 15 untouched course tabs at once.
